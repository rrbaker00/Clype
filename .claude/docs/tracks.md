# Sponsor tracks

Only one project allowed, but it can be entered for several tracks.

| Sponsor | Challenge | How we fit | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wordsmith** (main) | Most complicated end-to-end agentic browser-use workflow. Ideally legal. | Visit → reject → browse → read policy → compare → draft legal letter. | AirPods Max each |
| **G-Research** | Make the invisible visible. | Shows hidden tracking clearly. | Switch 2s |
| **Metaview** | Best project overall. | Just be good. | $500 each |
| **JetBrains** | Help the developer, AI solution. Judged partly on building with JetBrains Air agents. | Build in Air; pitch "devs can audit their own site before launch". | €350 each |

Weak / skipped: Bank of Scotland (only a loose "free costs you data" angle),
HRT, InstaLILY, Viridien.

## What judges care about
- **Wordsmith:** workflow complexity first. Also tokens used, model used,
  time to complete. Show these numbers in the demo.
- **G-Research:** what data is hidden, how we found it, what you can do with it.
- **JetBrains:** in order: it actually runs end-to-end, fits dev workflow,
  originality, agentic dev process in Air (multi-agent = extra credit), demo.

## Demo checklist
- 2–3 pre-tested sites where something juicy happens.
- Show the tokens/time numbers on screen.
- Recorded backup video in case wifi dies.
- Last 2 hours: no new features.
